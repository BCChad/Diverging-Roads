Diverging Roads

Chronicles of the Cornwall Iron Furnace, the Coleman family, and the people who built Lebanon Valley — one overlooked story at a time.

by Bruce Chadbourne

About this collection

Bruce Chadbourne didn't grow up in Lebanon County, but the Cornwall Iron Furnace, its ore banks, and the buildings left behind pulled him in early. The deeper he dug, the more the Coleman family's story kept turning up — threading through colonial Pennsylvania, the Civil War, and into the 20th century.

Diverging Roads is where those stories live: overlooked houses, forgotten letters, and the people whose lives quietly shaped the Lebanon Valley. This page collects the full run in one place, moved here from LebTown so it has a permanent home.
